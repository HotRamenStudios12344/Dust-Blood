ABOUT THE GAME

A hard country.
Hard choices.

Dust & Blood is a traditional Western tabletop roleplaying game focused on frontier life, dangerous encounters, and the choices that define a character.

Create your own character through Backgrounds, Core Scores, Proficiencies, Traits, and Flaws. Your story is shaped by the decisions you make at the table.
